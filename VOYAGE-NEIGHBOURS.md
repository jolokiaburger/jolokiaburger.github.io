# Voyage 0.16 · People across the water

Optional trips, favours, recipes and conversations give the existing nine quays more reasons to matter. No additional full location is introduced. Classic and the original assets remain intact. Save keys and format version stay at 1.

## Passenger trips

Open **People → Passenger trips**, **Harbour pastimes**, the Journal or Menu. Priya, Lam and Rin also offer their trip through their person view. Read the terms, choose a direct route or an optional thermos stop, then board deliberately.

| Passenger / route | Available from | Fare before fuel | Optional thermos stop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Priya · Landing 3 → Metro Quay | 00:50 | 18 cr | Kurage 33 |
| Lam · Metro Quay → Kisaragi | 01:40 | 22 cr | Night Market |
| Rin · Starling Yard → Kurage 33 | 00:30 | 20 cr | Night Market |

Boarding takes three game minutes. Disembarking at the named destination takes two and pays the fare exactly once. Docking alone never pays it. One new fare passenger can travel alongside Nao or Yumi; the fare uses no freight slot. Each person has a visible deck figure, luggage and crossing observations.

A thermos stop is free and takes eight minutes at its quay. It is optional even after agreement: change the route before sharing it, or take your passenger straight home. Fuel, refills and detours remain the captain's costs; the fare is not promised net profit. There is no separate delivery deadline, but the full action must fit by 06:00. Waiting for a passenger's release is an explicit, free time advance at their boarding quay. A fare may be closed at any quay without payment. Each passenger's fare is available once per voyage, including closed fares. Successful trips produce honest follow-up letters when the voyage is recorded.

## Neighbour favours

Read the harbour board or choose **Neighbour favours**. Accepting is free at any quay. Small recipe packets use no freight slot and cost nothing. All four can be followed in the Journal; closing an unfinished favour is free at a quay and awards nothing.

| Favour | Steps | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supper for the cook | Give Mei one kept fish at Kurage · 4 min | Fish becomes her supper; no cash |
| Tea for the late crew | Pack Jun's flask at Kisaragi · 5 min; deliver to Matte at Frostline · 5 min | 8 cr thanks before fuel |
| A picnic under the charts | Pack Mei's rice at Kurage and Jun's tea at Kisaragi · 5 min each, either order; deliver to Mio · 5 min | Shared picnic; no cash |
| The twice-rung bell | Read Priya's bell register and Mako's bracket account; compare at Landing 3 · 3 min | Choose the checked repair account or a clearly labelled legend |

Bell comparison requires both conversations in this voyage. Both interpretations are valid choices. The resolved board post keeps the maintenance record separate from folklore, and the Journal remembers the actual decision. These are original harbour stories; they grant no hidden market information or automatic dawn payment.

## Nao's recipe notebook

Ask the named teacher about a recipe through **Ask about the harbour · Free**, then open the notebook. Learned pages persist between voyages. Cook at Nao's Night Market counter when she is back at work. One serving of each recipe is available per voyage. Nao supplies the other ingredients: the captain supplies one explicitly selected kept fish for each fish bowl.

| Recipe / teacher | Broth | Garnish | Tea | Kept fish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lantern catch bowl · Mei | Gentle miso | Spring greens | Roasted | One |
| Rain-window ginger bowl · Nao | Ginger | Silken tofu | Black | One |
| Canal garden supper · Jun | Gentle miso | Roasted mushrooms | Jasmine | None |

Choose broth, garnish and tea on the illustrated tray; fish bowls also require a catch selection. Ingredients are a free draft until **Share the bowl**. Leaving the tray spends nothing. Serving takes six minutes, costs no credits and consumes that fish once. Variations receive their own friendly response; there is no failure penalty or cash reward. Recipes and Nao's existing three-customer shift are distinct activities. Favourite matched customer combinations from that shift are remembered in the notebook and kitchen.

The fishing basket still has three slots and six landed catches per voyage. Cooked or gifted fish leave the kept basket and cannot be sold, released or served again. Their species discoveries remain in the fishing notebook.

## Conversations and useful knowledge

Each of the fourteen neighbours has two optional topics, 28 in total. Open a person's **Ask about the harbour** section inside the port panel. Choose a topic and then a fully written player reply. The NPC's response follows that choice, with selected exchanges noticing completed stories. Existing chat and quest actions remain available. Topics do not appear automatically on startup or docking.

Reading and replying are free and pause sailing. First-heard attribution and the most recent reply are retained in this voyage. The facts notebook remembers learned topics across voyages; older memories are identified without assigning a new hearing time. Its filters distinguish working rules, history/rumours and recipes/science. Talking never observes a remote gold scale or discloses a future quote.

The practical topics explain actual game rules: spreads, quote age, cargo capacity, fuel pumps, full-duration dawn limits, upgrade costs and explicit passenger fares. Bell lore is labelled as local history or rumour and can be checked with Mako. An evidence-dependent reply tells the player to ask Mako first instead of presenting evidence they have not obtained.

Two optional science notes are original paraphrases with primary-source links in the notebook:

- Kenji: gold's resistance to corrosion and its use in reliable electrical contacts. Background: [USGS, Gold Fact Sheet, 2002](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2002/of02-303/OFR_02-303.pdf). The Basin's gold economy remains fictional.
- Mio: a constellation is a pattern viewed from Earth, whose stars can be at very different distances. Background: [NASA, Discovering the Universe Through the Constellation Orion](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/stories/quick-reads/discovering-the-universe-through-the-constellation-orion/).

The user's design brief drew on [Sunless Sea](https://www.failbettergames.com/games/sunless-sea), [Dredge](https://www.dredge.game/press-kit), Deus Ex's readable conversation choices and food/adventure anime's shared meals. This adaptation uses short authored crossings, local evidence, optional knowledgeable neighbours and cooking with actual catches. The cast, dialogue, bell story and new artwork are original to Neon Tides. No game dialogue, characters, screenshots or anime panels are bundled.

## A patient landing

**Passenger trips → Docking practice** switches the optional guide on or off. Near a quay it shows approach feedback, a dotted approach line and a mooring ring. Ease off and align the bow until the guide turns green, then choose **Dock**. A gentle approach earns a record; a quicker or misaligned arrival simply says **Secured alongside**. There is no damage, fee, fuel change or extra clock cost. Existing assisted sailing and docking remain available, and practice starts off for older saves. The preference persists for fresh voyages.

The native check uses speed at most 18 map units/second and a heading difference at most 0.8 radians towards the quay. This is a cosmetic practice assessment, not a new collision rule. The normal docking radius is unchanged. Up to 60 landing records are retained in a voyage; the profile remembers distinct gentle quays.

## Save compatibility and source files

The voyage's optional `neighbours` record holds bounded rides, favours, learned recipe IDs, servings, attributed topics, chosen replies, a practice preference and landing records. The profile's optional `neighbours` record remembers owned topic/recipe IDs, cooked recipes, completed trips/favours, gentle quays, customer preferences and practice. Older saves pass validation before empty defaults are added. Loading also reconciles learning already recorded in the current voyage with its profile; this creates no completed jobs or fares.

Validators check owned IDs, unique records, valid times, canonical payments, permitted choices and the actual fish linked to a serving/gift. Orphan consumed fish and reused catches are rejected. The original unreadable-save preservation and separate Classic key remain. A new voyage carries learning and cosmetics, not money, completed jobs, fish or passenger records. No startup guide or pending activity is forced open.
