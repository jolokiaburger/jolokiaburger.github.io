Trade gold. Follow the lanterns.
Find your people.

Captain the Tern through a rain-soaked neon harbour. Buy and sell, carry a little cargo, and share food, tea and stories with the people along your route.

Two nights & a morning chapter · About 10–15 minutes each
Your three-chapter journey

Night One · Find your footing. Listen to harbour rumours, compare gold prices, explore the market and help Nao open her breakfast counter.

Night Two · A Lantern for Tomorrow. Carry your earnings and relationships forward. Choose Nao’s recipe, trade sealed rice cargo, and follow her invitation to Kisaragi Canal Town. Help her choose a small festival table or an afternoon off.

Chapter Three · Morning After the Lanterns. Share breakfast, help Rin with a stranded festival delivery and follow Priya’s revised route. Your choices leave memories in the harbour. Explore the busy salvage yard or plan a small cargo trade.

Stay for a meal, chat without spending a credit, or take the long way home. Discover the canal route at Landing 3, trade tea with Jun and solve Mako’s missing address. Gentle harbour sounds and lo-fi radio are waiting in the Menu, off until you choose them.
