# Player guide

**Applies to:** Voyage 0.17 and Classic 4.6.1.

Neon Tides begins at Kurage 33 aboard the ferry *Tern*. Choose a mode on the first page, then explore the harbour through deliveries, trading and conversations. Food and tea offer places to pause between crossings.

## Voyage: your first crossing

1. Open **People** at Kurage 33 and speak to Mei. Collect Nao's free tea case if you want a short introductory delivery.
2. Choose **Night Market** under **Set course**, review the preview and select **Cast off & set course**. Assisted sailing follows a water route; WASD, arrows or the held direction buttons provide manual steering.
3. Sail inside the quay's dotted ring and choose **Dock**. Reaching a course destination does not dock automatically.
4. Speak to Nao and explicitly hand over the case for 24 cr. The Journal records the completed delivery.
5. Check the tank and purse before choosing the next crossing. Fuel pumps fill the six-unit tank for 30 cr and ten game minutes.

The tea delivery is optional. **Menu → How to play** reopens guidance at any time. Startup leaves the harbour controls available. The orange **Docked at…** message is an arrival summary; its six-second countdown uses real time and changes no voyage resources.

## Time, fuel and recovery

A Voyage night runs from 23:40 to 06:00. Sailing and labelled activities advance game time. Reading, trading, talking and open panels pause it; hidden tabs do not advance the simulation. Activities must fit their full duration before dawn. An action may finish exactly at 06:00.

Use the journey strip and route previews to check arrival time, fuel and delivery windows. When a plan names a fuel pump, set course to that pump yourself. Estimates need allowance for manual turns and detours.

Fuel is available at Landing 3, Starling Yard, Kisaragi and Tsukimi, and at both coast quays after they unlock. The harbour tug recovers a stranded ferry to Landing 3 for up to 20 cr and twenty minutes; the final-minute recovery shows its actual remaining duration. The fee is bounded by your purse.

## Gold and cargo

A scale has two prices per gram: **Buy** is the amount you pay; **Sell** is the amount you receive. Selling normally pays less than buying at the same scale. Prices vary by quay, clock and the night's events. Dealers have finite stock and buying allowances, and your trades affect their quotes.

The Journal records quotes you have observed, with their age. A remembered quote is historical information. Conversations may explain a market or suggest a lead without providing a current remote price.

Cargo offers show purchase cost, promised payment, destination and handover window. **Gross margin** excludes fuel and other spending. The detailed trip budget includes required new fuel purchases where a workable route exists. Starting fuel was already aboard and is excluded from estimated net cash. Deliver explicitly to receive payment; reaching the quay or dawn alone grants no payout.

The normal freight hold has two slots. Kenji's cargo racks add a third. Gold, passengers, recipe packets and the fishing basket have separate rules. Expired cargo can be unloaded at a quay without payment or refund.

## People and activities

| Activity | Where to open it | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passenger trips | People, Menu or Journal | Read a fare, board deliberately and disembark at the named destination; tea detours are optional |
| Neighbour favours | Harbour board, Menu or Journal | Small deliveries and an investigation with tracked steps |
| Harbour fishing | Menu → Harbour pastimes while moored | Four game minutes per landed catch; the bite waits for you, or Relaxed mode reels automatically |
| Nao's kitchen shift | Night Market while Nao is working | Choose broth, topping and tea for three customers; only serving commits a tray |
| Recipes | Character topics, then Nao's recipe notebook | Learned recipes persist; fish bowls require an actual kept catch |
| Photo errands | Yumi at Metro or Mio at Tsukimi | Accept a request, sail near its view, capture it and return to the requester |
| Docking practice | Passenger trips → Docking practice | Optional approach guidance and cosmetic records, without a damage or fee penalty |
| Closer dockside seats | People at supported quays | Illustrated views with conversation, food and activity controls |

All costs and durations appear before a committed activity. Leaving an ingredient tray spends nothing. The Journal keeps active stories, postcards and earned ferry decorations. Decorations change appearance only.

Character topics present complete player replies and attribute notebook entries to their speaker. Working rules, history, rumours, recipes and science have distinct labels. The twice-rung bell story can be checked against both Priya's and Mako's accounts.

## Beyond the Breakwater

At Nao's Night Market counter, choose **People → Beyond the Breakwater → Read Nao's invitation**. Prepare ingredients with Mei and weatherproof trim with Rin. Complete a night to access **Continue the expedition** on its report.

The expedition opens Aonami Tea Island and Shiosai Coastal Station in its second chapter. Invite Nao and Rin aboard, follow the recipe case and station notebook, then bring them home for a final supper. Conversations can reveal a surveyed inlet, another tasting and a different ending. **Chart** provides named destination controls, route previews, pins and an enlarged view.

Continuation keeps your actual purse, gold purchase basis, fuel, fittings and owned sealed cargo. An unfinished chapter can use another night. Delivery windows renew as stated in the continuation terms; completed contracts do not pay again. Finite daily activities reset. If the night ends at sea, read the explicit overnight tug terms before continuing. See [VOYAGE-BREAKWATER.md](VOYAGE-BREAKWATER.md) for detailed rules and story spoilers.

## Classic

Classic uses destination choices rather than direct steering. Read each crossing's fuel and time cost before selecting it. Use the ringed scene targets or the matching action buttons; the same action has the same cost in either place.

The trading season has three chapters: the first gold night, **A Lantern for Tomorrow**, and **Morning After the Lanterns**. Continue through each shift report to carry earnings, gold purchase history, fuel and story memories. Case files on the title screen open four separate investigations.

Talking and reading are free. Some food conversations reveal trading leads. The notebook preserves the wording, speaker and time of evidence or rumours. In case files, compare evidence with the timeline, return to the person whose account you can challenge, then support an explanation with a held clue. Menu → Hints changes progress guidance.

## Sound, motion and saves

Sound begins muted. Tune the radio and enable harbour sounds separately; volume and sound preferences are remembered. Reopening may require a tap before sound starts. Motion settings and system reduced-motion preferences control decorative movement.

Progress saves in the current browser. Voyage and Classic use separate saves; each mode's new-game action affects its own progression. A fresh Voyage keeps profile learning and owned cosmetics, while an expedition continuation carries its current resources. Storage restrictions allow session play but may prevent persistence; follow the displayed notice. Clearing site data removes local saves and preferences. Local development and the public site have separate save origins.
