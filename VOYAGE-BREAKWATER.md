# Voyage 0.17 · Beyond the Breakwater

An optional Nao/Rin adventure in three chapters, with a compact outer coast. The home harbour remains available. Two new quays extend the map eastwards to 5100 × 1800; the original nine quay IDs and coordinates are unchanged. Classic and existing save keys/version remain intact.

## Begin and continue

At Nao’s Night Market counter, choose **People → Beyond the Breakwater → Read Nao’s invitation**. Reading and acceptance are free. The story never opens automatically on startup or docking. It can begin in an existing Voyage save. Bring Nao to her counter first if she is on an earlier outing.

| Chapter | Required steps | Time / cash |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · An invitation folded into a chart | Choose Nao’s herb bowl or smoked rice; pack Mei’s dry ingredients at Kurage; fit Rin’s weatherproof trim at the yard | Choice free; pack 6 min / 8 cr; fitting 10 min / free |
| 2 · The lantern that answers | Invite Nao/Rin at the Market; taste at Aonami; recover the labelled waterproof recipe case; restore the cooker at Shiosai | Board 5 min; tasting 6 min; case 6 min; cooker 8 min; all free |
| 3 · A table with a homeward view | Sail the companions home to Nao’s counter; choose the supper’s future; share it | Homecoming/choice free; supper 12 min / free |

Finish the night deliberately through the notebook or Services, or reach dawn. The report offers **Continue the expedition**. A ready chapter advances; an unfinished chapter remains available on another night. A night still runs from 23:40 to 06:00. All activities require their full duration before dawn; exact-dawn completion is allowed. The three chapters may take more than three nights. At most 99 expedition nights are accepted by the bounded save.

The original harbour’s jobs and stories remain playable throughout. Companions use no cargo slot, rotate deck observations and appear with their own portraits in People. They stay aboard for the real homeward crossing between chapters two and three, and leave after the completed supper. Engine tuning, cargo racks and the expedition fitting carry into subsequent expedition nights. Rin’s fitting reduces actual fuel per distance by 10%, stacking with the existing tuned engine; it changes no steering geometry or clock rate.

## What carries overnight

Continuation retains the actual purse, owned gold lots and their purchase basis, tank contents, boat upgrades, story flags, visited quays, settled discoveries, chosen menu, practical notes and route choice. A moored continuation begins at the same quay and grants no fuel. Cosmetics, recipes and earlier character memories retain their existing profile behaviour.

Sealed paid cargo and unfinished courier/letter cases remain aboard, sharing the same two/three-slot hold. Their desk windows renew for the next night. Collected goods are not purchased again, and completed contract records remain terminal. No earlier contract is paid again. The current-night cash ledger starts at the carried purse/stock; the illustrated result subtracts carried delivery/discovery/courier receipts instead of reporting them as new income. Delivery/story details retain their expedition-wide outcomes and are labelled accordingly.

Finite ordinary pastimes start a new night with their normal empty records. Fare passengers take an overnight connection without an unpaid fare or a claimed destination homecoming. Coastal catches are shared with the overnight crew, and the expedition basket starts empty. These terms are shown before continuation.

If finishing at sea, continuation explicitly recovers to Landing 3 by an overnight tug. The fee is up to 20 cr, bounded by the purse; recovery leaves at least three fuel, following the existing tug’s recovery allowance. The fee is included in the next night’s opening purse and log. A penniless captain can continue. Starting an ordinary fresh voyage remains the separate existing reset action, with confirmation; it does not transfer expedition cash or silently complete chapters.

## Knowledge changes your choices

New conversations are fully authored, with complete player replies and a matching NPC response. They cost no time or credits. Each account requires the named character at its quay, and some require the recovered pages. The notebook records the choice; facts do not observe a remote gold scale.

- **Nao at the Market:** choose the travelling menu before packing. The menu is then fixed for the expedition and remains in the chapter and final letter.
- **Priya at Landing 3:** ask her to mark the surveyed inlet. This unlocks a direct safe coastal course. The default sheltered route follows the outer buoy detour. Their actual waypoint geometry produces different sailing time and fuel; no unexplained speed buff is applied. Choose a route while moored, inspect its preview and pin it on the chart. Manual steering remains available.
- **Jun at Kisaragi:** ask what to tell the growers. His introduction unlocks a leaf-steamed rice tasting at Aonami, alongside the usual brewed-tea pairing. Both take six minutes, free, and the selected method is saved.
- **Mio at Tsukimi:** discuss the outer lamp’s test sequence. Her account and the physical signal tag give the light a checked context.
- **Rin at Shiosai:** after recovering the case, read Etsuko’s last notebook page. It opens the station-kitchen ending. The travelling supper book is available as the other future.

Both endings complete the adventure without a cash award or punishment. Nao credits the growers and Etsuko; Rin’s letter reflects the chosen future. The kitchen ending gives its rota to the crew, and the book ending carries named methods to another route. Neither requires Nao or Rin to become a permanent keeper. These are original fictional people and events.

## Decisions on the water

Three optional coastal stops appear on the chart and have direct course controls:

- **The lantern that answers:** inspect its test tag, free / five minutes. It records the signal’s ordinary purpose.
- **A waterproof recipe case:** retrieve Etsuko’s labelled pages, free / six minutes. This is the chapter’s required water stop.
- **The supper skiff:** share the crew’s tea, free / six minutes, and hear its preparation method.

Leaving a coast stop is free and leaves it available for another crossing. Settled stops remain marked and cannot grant another reward or duplicate story steps. Coastal exploration requires the expedition companions in chapter two. None opens a window automatically.

**Coastal fishing** appears while sailing in unlocked outer water. The Tern holds position while fishing; each catch takes four game minutes, with three catches per night. Moon mullet and ribbon mackerel are fictional coastal species, held in a separate expedition basket. Share a kept catch with the cooks at Aonami or Nao’s Market counter, free / four minutes; it can be shared once. There is no sale, automatic payment or full-hold penalty. Existing dockside fishing remains available at both new quays using its normal notebook, basket and rules.

## Illustrated chart, scenery and controls

Open **Chart** in the header, **Illustrated region chart** under People/Journal, or the expedition notebook. The chart uses the exact navigation data, with native paper colours, water hatching, illustrated landmarks and a north marker. Red marks the Tern; green marks visited quays; amber marks charted quays; squares mark coastal discoveries. Unknown coast content remains covered until unlocked.

Named controls provide all destinations without clicking the picture. Preview actual sailing minutes, fuel and pump availability; pin a route to draw its waypoint line. The **Enlarge chart** view pans for detail on phones and can return to Fit. Closing a preview commits no course. Course buttons explicitly cast off where appropriate, and stop at the destination without automatic docking.

The new `voyage/coast-art.js` draws tea terraces, station structures, cooker/table details, lamps and coastal markers. The Tern carries Nao/Rin figures and expedition luggage. Two optional 1440 × 960 WebP seats are `assets/scenes/aonami-dockside.webp` and `assets/scenes/shiosai-dockside.webp`; both use the game’s own identities and established warm ink/painted style. Built-in image generation supplied them; Pillow only resized and exported. Exact prompts, references and hashes are in `tools/voyage-17-art-prompts.json`. Original images remain. Decorative steam respects reduced motion; physics and chart data are unchanged by motion settings.
